Saidu Kalokoh

Hello, I'm Saidu 👋

I am Saidu Kalokoh, a young technology enthusiast and aspiring developer with a strong interest in computers, programming and digital innovation.

My journey into technology is driven by curiosity, creativity and the desire to learn how digital tools can solve real problems and create opportunities for young people.

Codeskul Computer Studies is part of that journey — a place where computer knowledge can be explained in a simple, practical and student-friendly way.

About Me

My goal is not simply to use technology, but to understand it, build with it and use it to create useful solutions.

I am continuously developing my knowledge through practice, experimentation and personal projects. I believe that technology becomes more powerful when young people are given the opportunity to learn how it works and how they can create with it.

Why I Created Codeskul Computer Studies

I created Codeskul Computer Studies because I believe that learning computer studies should be more than memorising definitions from a textbook.

Students should be able to understand concepts, see practical examples and develop the confidence to use computers themselves.

Codeskul computer studies was created to provide simple explanations, practical activities and beginner-friendly computer lessons that can help students move from simply using technology to understanding and eventually creating with technology.
